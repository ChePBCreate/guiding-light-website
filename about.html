About

After experiencing a spiritual awakening after losing his father in 2001, Paul Gerald has made it his mission to help others find clarity, purpose, and connection during their life's journey. With over 20 years of experience as a life coach, mentor, and spiritual guide, Paul has helped countless clients navigate life's challenges and create the fulfilling life they deserve.

Skilled in tarot reading, dream interpretation, spiritual guidance, and energetically in tune- Paul has taken his specialties and turned them into a comprehensive approach to helping others find clarity and purpose in their lives globally- from New York, to Dubai, to right in your pocket.

Whether you're navigating a major life transition, seeking clarity in your career, curious about love, or simply looking to deepen your spiritual connection, Paul is here to assist with wisdom, compassion, and practical tools.
